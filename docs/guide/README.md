# Melodex guide

Melodex finds what's missing from your music library, fetches it through plugins you choose, files it with beets,
and tells your media server. It runs beside Navidrome, or on its own: Subsonic players can play your library
straight from Melodex.

These pages are also in the web app, under **Settings → Help**.

1. [Installing](installing.md): Docker, the folders Melodex needs, and the first start
2. [Getting started](getting-started.md): the setup steps, your first scan, and what the library shows
3. [Requests and listening](requests-and-listening.md): fetching music, the Activity page, and Listen now
4. [Playlists](playlists.md): making them, folders, and syncing them with Navidrome
5. [Subsonic players](subsonic-players.md): playing your library in Symfonium, Amperfy, Feishin and other Subsonic apps
6. [Your library](library.md): Review, files that don't match, your own music, formatting and removing
7. [Tools](tools.md): importing lists of songs and Spotify or YouTube Music playlists, and exporting playlists and the library
8. [Users and permissions](users-and-permissions.md): accounts, what each permission allows, and API keys
9. [Appearance](appearance.md): themes, light and dark, and high contrast
10. [Plugins](plugins.md): Navidrome, retrieval plugins, catalogue and artwork sources
11. [Configuration](configuration.md): environment variables, ports and paths
12. [Upgrading](upgrading.md): new versions, and moving from Navidex
13. [Troubleshooting](troubleshooting.md): when something doesn't work
